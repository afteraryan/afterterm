When the tour shows and what it leaves behind

  • Once, on the first launch after updating to the version that brings loops, on Home. Never again after Done or Skip; a "Take the tour" entry in What's new can play it again.
  • Someone who just updated has no loops, so the tour fills Progress with example loops and an example calendar, slightly faded, with an "Example" tag. They go away when the tour ends and Home shows the real state.
  • Someone who already has loops or calendar days sees their own, not examples.
  • If Progress was closed, the tour opens it first.
  • Inside afterterm's own window only: no new window, nothing takes focus from a terminal.
  • The loops button shows in the workspace header even with no thread open (today it only shows with a thread; the build adds it).
  • Every card has a picture: a piece of afterterm's own UI, enlarged and tilted on a deeper blue, with a cursor showing the action.
