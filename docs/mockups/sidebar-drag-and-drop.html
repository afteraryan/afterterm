Dragging in the sidebar moves projects and threads, with a line showing where they land

  • Drag a project up or down inside Pinned or Recent, including above the first one.
  • Drag a project from Recent into Pinned to pin it, or back down to unpin it.
  • Drag a thread between two threads, onto another project, or above Pinned to take it out of its project.
  • A thread moving to another project shows where from and where to on its card. Two projects have long names to show how the line shortens.
  • Scroll the list with the wheel, or hold a dragged row near the top or bottom edge.
