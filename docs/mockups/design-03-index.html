Six questions about the sidebar

Each question opens a page with two or three answers and a picture of each. Click the answers at the top of the page to compare them, press This is my pick on the one you want, then Next. Your picks are remembered on this computer. When all six are answered, come back here and press Copy answers, then paste the text into the chat.

1. Which projects show up on the rail (the thin strip at the far left)?Only the ones that need me, all of them, or pinned plus the ones that need me. 2. Which projects are listed in the sidebar?All of them, only recent ones, or only the ones I opened. 3. How do I get to the threads that need me or are working?Tabs at the top, a block that is always there, or chips along the top of the window. 4. How do pinned projects look different from the rest?A line, a line plus a pin icon, or a lighter box. 5. Where is the button that collapses every project?Top of the sidebar, on the group labels, or both. 6. What happens when I type in the Search box?The list shrinks, the palette opens, or keep today's button.
